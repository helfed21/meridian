Process

Six stages, the same for a single apartment or a hotel. You always know what comes next and what you'll receive at the end of each stage.

  1. 1

    Discovery

    We visit the site, walk through how you live and agree on scope, budget and timing.

    You receiveA written brief and fee proposal
  2. 2

    Concept

    Sketches, massing and a first table of materials. We settle the direction before any detail.

    You receiveConcept book with plans and a material palette
  3. 3

    Design development

    Every room drawn in detail: joinery, lighting, stone, fabrics. Planning applications go in here.

    You receiveFull drawing set and a costed specification
  4. 4

    Craft and sourcing

    Our Warsaw workshop builds the joinery and furniture; we source art and objects in parallel.

    You receiveSamples and workshop visits, in person or by video
  5. 5

    Execution

    We run the build with our own site team and trusted contractors, reporting to you weekly.

    You receiveA weekly update and one point of contact
  6. 6

    Reveal and aftercare

    We style the rooms, hand over the keys and come back after a year to check how everything has settled.

    You receiveA home ready to live in and a maintenance guide
