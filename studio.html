Three ateliers, one way of making a room quiet

Our story

Meridian began as a single project shared between an architect in London, an interior designer in Copenhagen and a workshop in Warsaw. It went well enough that we never stopped.

Each atelier leads the part it knows best. London handles planning, structure and listed buildings. Copenhagen shapes how a home is lived in, room by room.

Warsaw builds what we draw: joinery, furniture, stonework. Because we make it ourselves, we can promise how it will look in twenty years.

Materials first

Every project starts with a table of samples, not a floor plan. We choose stone, timber and textiles that age well and feel right to the hand, then design around them.
