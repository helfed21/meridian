Harbour House

Location
Copenhagen
Type
Residential
Year
2024
Area
640 m²
The brief

A family of five wanted a former warehouse on the harbour to feel like a house, not a loft, without losing the view of the water.

Concept

We divided the open floor with three oak volumes: a kitchen, a library and a stair. Each one is a piece of furniture scaled to a room, so the ceiling height stays readable from every corner.

The palette follows the harbour: pale limestone, grey-washed oak and linen that picks up the light off the water.

Location

Christianshavn, on the canal side of the harbour. Low sun from the west for most of the year shaped where every window went.

Services used

ArchitectureInterior designArt and object curationTurnkey realisation

Materials and craft

Joinery made in our Warsaw workshop, stone from a single quarry on Öland.

The result

Five bedrooms, a library and a kitchen that opens onto the quay. The warehouse beams and brick stayed; everything added can be taken out again without trace.

“The children call the oak stair ‘the tree’. That was the moment we knew it worked.”

Mette and Jonas Lindqvist, owners
